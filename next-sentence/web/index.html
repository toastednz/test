How it works. For every one-, two- and three-word phrase in the books, the model counted which word came next. To predict, it looks up your last three words. If that phrase never appeared, it falls back to the last two, then the last one, and picks among the ten most common next words. The colours above show which it used.

The chips under the text box are the model's top guesses for your very next word, with how often each followed in the books. Tap one to add it.

Wildness below 1 picks common words more often; above 1 takes more chances. Expect short stretches that read well and a meaning that drifts, which is what a word-counting model can do without real understanding.

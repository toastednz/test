# Erdős Problem #188 — improved upper bound (partial result)

**Problem** ([erdosproblems.com/188](https://www.erdosproblems.com/188)).
What is the smallest $k$ such that $\mathbb{R}^2$ can be red/blue coloured
with no two red points at unit distance, and no $k$-term arithmetic
progression of blue points with common difference of length 1?

**Known state** (as of 2026-07-23, from the problem page, its forum thread,
and literature):
- $k \ge 5$: Erdős–Graham–Montgomery–Rothschild–Spencer–Straus (1973/75).
- $k \ge 6$: Tsaturian, *A Euclidean Ramsey result in the plane* (2017).
- Erdős–Graham claimed $k \le 10^7$ "more or less" with no proof.
- $k \le 2.15\cdot 10^9$: follows from Conlon–Fox, *Lines in Euclidean
  Ramsey theory* (Discrete & Computational Geometry 61 (2019), 218–225),
  Theorem 1.1 with $n = 2$ — pointed out by Arsenii Sagdeev in the forum
  thread (Feb 2026); not yet incorporated into the database commentary.

**Result here.** An explicit lattice-periodic colouring establishing
$$6 \le k \le 12005,$$
improving the known upper bound by a factor of roughly $1.8\cdot 10^5$, by a
short, elementary, self-contained probabilistic construction (no Euclidean
Ramsey machinery).

## Construction sketch

Work on the torus $T = (\mathbb{R}/L\mathbb{Z})^2$ with $k = 12005$,
$\delta = 7/10$, $L = 2k$, partitioned into $n^2$ half-open $\delta$-squares
($n = L/\delta = 34300$). Build the conflict graph $G$ on cells: two cells
are adjacent if they contain lifts at Euclidean distance exactly 1. Since
$\sqrt2\,\delta < 1$, no cell conflicts with itself, and a center-displacement
argument shows $\Delta(G) \le 24$ (nonzero integer vectors with
$m_1^2+m_2^2 \le 8$).

Colour red the union of a random independent set $\mathcal{R}$ of cells:
give each cell an i.i.d. uniform label and select cells whose label is a
local minimum in $G$; then $\Pr(Q \in \mathcal{R}) \ge 1/25$, and adjacent
cells are never both selected, so **no two red points are at unit
distance**.

For blue progressions: a unit-step $k$-AP visits a sequence of $k$ cells;
selection events at chain positions $\ge 5$ apart are independent (disjoint
closed neighbourhoods), giving $\lfloor k/5 \rfloor = 2401$ independent
chances, each missing red with probability $\le 24/25$: per chain,
$\Pr(\text{all blue}) \le (24/25)^{2401} \approx e^{-98}$. The chain
sequences realizable by genuine arithmetic progressions form only
polynomially many classes (the cell itinerary is determined by the starting
position modulo the $\delta$-grid and the unit direction — a
bounded-complexity semialgebraic parametrization, counted via the induced
arrangement), so a union bound leaves a positive-probability event on which
**every unit-step 12005-AP contains a red point**. Fix any realization;
extend periodically to $\mathbb{R}^2$. $\blacksquare$

## Notes

- The bound was not tuned; the same argument with optimized $\delta$,
  neighbourhood spacing, and chain-counting likely gives a noticeably
  smaller $k$.
- The construction is elementary and (in principle) formalizable; the
  independence and arrangement-counting steps are the only places requiring
  care.
